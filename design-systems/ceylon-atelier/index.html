The visual language of the POSTCARD.LK gallery-marketplace — a curated, editorial commerce system rooted in Sri Lankan heritage. Emerald, brass & terracotta on warm paper.

00
Principles
  • Gallery, not shop. Artwork sits in mats and frames; generous space; restrained chrome.
  • Warm & tactile. Aged-paper ground with a subtle dotted grid; nothing pure white or cold.
  • Editorial serif voice. Newsreader for headings & prices; Hanken Grotesk for everything functional.
  • Three inks, used with intent. Terracotta acts (primary CTAs), brass elevates (checkout & accents), emerald grounds (footers & summaries).
  • Multilingual by default. English · සිංහල · தமிழ் — Noto Sinhala/Tamil fonts, relaxed tracking on Indic scripts.
